# ⚡ StreamDisplay Assistant v1.0.0 — Initial Public Release

Welcome to the official **v1.0.0 release** of **StreamDisplay Assistant**!

StreamDisplay Assistant is a standalone, lightweight Windows 10/11 utility designed for PC gamers streaming to **iPad Air/Pro**, **Steam Deck**, **ROG Ally**, and handhelds using **Sunshine**, **Moonlight**, **Steam Remote Play**, or **Parsec**.

---

### ✨ Highlights & Features in v1.0.0

- 🎯 **Zero Black Bars & Auto-Adaptation**: Automatically matches incoming client resolutions (e.g. 1920x1440 @ 180Hz for iPad Air M2 4:3, 1920x1200 for Steam Deck 16:10) with razor-sharp rendering.
- 🖤 **Multi-Monitor OLED Blackout Shield**: Blankets all physical monitors with pure `#000000` black (0 nits), powering down OLED subpixels completely to prevent burn-in and reduce PC power draw by ~75W.
- 🔇 **Host Audio Auto-Mute**: Automatically mutes desktop PC speakers while streaming and restores original volume upon disconnect.
- 🌈 **Windows HDR Automation**: Toggles Windows HDR on during streaming and turns it back off when done.
- ⌨️ **System Emergency Hotkeys**:
  - `Ctrl + Shift + F12`: Emergency Panic Button -> Restore Physical Native Resolution
  - `Ctrl + Shift + F11`: Toggle OLED Blackout Shield
  - `Ctrl + Shift + H`: Toggle Windows HDR
  - `Ctrl + Shift + M`: Toggle Host Audio Mute
- 📊 **OLED Protection & Eco Dashboard**: Tracks protected streaming sessions, hours of display wear eliminated, and kWh saved.
- 🔍 **Multi-Drive Streaming Discovery**: Auto-detects 10 major streaming hosts (Sunshine, Steam, Parsec, Moonlight, AnyDesk, RustDesk, TeamViewer, Chiaki, OBS) across C:, D:, E:, etc.
- 🛡️ **100% Native Architecture**: Zero third-party drivers or invasive DLLs. 100% Anti-Cheat safe (Valorant Vanguard, EasyAntiCheat, BattlEye).
